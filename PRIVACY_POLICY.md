# Privacy Policy - FaddedSMS

**Last Updated**: October 7, 2025

## 1. Introduction

FaddedSMS ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number
- **Transaction Data**: Purchase history, wallet balance, transaction records
- **Payment Information**: Bank transfer details (we do not store card numbers)
- **API Usage**: API keys, usage logs, endpoint access

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Log Data**: IP address, browser type, access times
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Cookies**: Session cookies, preference cookies

### 2.3 Third-Party Information
- **SMS Providers**: Order information shared with SMS providers
- **VTU Providers**: Transaction details for airtime, data, electricity

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 To Provide Services
- Process transactions (SMS, airtime, data, electricity)
- Manage your wallet and payments
- Send notifications and receipts
- Provide customer support

### 3.2 To Improve Services
- Analyze usage patterns
- Improve user experience
- Develop new features
- Fix bugs and issues

### 3.3 To Communicate
- Send transaction confirmations
- Notify about account activity
- Respond to inquiries
- Send important updates (opt-out available for marketing)

### 3.4 Security and Fraud Prevention
- Verify identity
- Detect fraudulent transactions
- Prevent unauthorized access
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Information Sharing

### 4.1 We Share Information With:

**Service Providers**:
- SMS providers (Tiger SMS, 5SIM, Dassy, TextVerified, SmsPool)
- VTU provider (VTU.ng)
- Payment processor (PayVibe)

**Legal Requirements**:
- Law enforcement (when legally required)
- Court orders
- Regulatory authorities

### 4.2 We DO NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share with third parties for marketing
- Provide data to data brokers
- Share without your consent (except as stated above)

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
- **Encryption**: HTTPS/SSL for all connections
- **Password Hashing**: Bcrypt for password storage
- **API Keys**: Secure token generation
- **Access Control**: Role-based permissions
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring

### 5.2 Data Storage
- **Location**: Secure servers
- **Backups**: Regular automated backups
- **Retention**: Data kept as long as account is active
- **Deletion**: Account deletion available on request

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 You Have the Right To:
- **Access**: Request copy of your data
- **Correction**: Update incorrect information
- **Deletion**: Request account deletion
- **Export**: Download your data
- **Opt-out**: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

### 6.2 How to Exercise Rights:
- Email: support@fadsms.com
- Settings: Update directly in your account
- Contact: Submit support ticket

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

### 7.1 We Use Cookies For:
- **Session Management**: Keep you logged in
- **Preferences**: Remember your settings (theme, language)
- **Analytics**: Understand usage patterns
- **Security**: Detect suspicious activity

### 7.2 Cookie Types:
- **Essential**: Required for site functionality
- **Functional**: Remember your preferences
- **Analytics**: Anonymous usage statistics

### 7.3 Managing Cookies:
- Browser settings to block cookies
- May affect site functionality
- Essential cookies always enabled

## 8. Third-Party Services

### 8.1 SMS Providers
We share order information with SMS providers to deliver services. Each provider has their own privacy policy.

### 8.2 Payment Processor
PayVibe processes payments. We share transaction amounts and references (not full bank details).

### 8.3 VTU Provider
VTU.ng processes airtime, data, and electricity purchases. We share meter numbers, phone numbers, and amounts.

## 9. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Retained for 2 years, then deleted
- **Transaction Records**: Kept for 7 years (legal requirement)
- **API Logs**: Retained for 90 days

## 10. Children's Privacy

- Service not intended for users under 18
- We do not knowingly collect data from minors
- Parent/guardian consent required for users under 18

## 11. International Users

- Service primarily for Nigerian users
- Data may be transferred internationally
- Adequate protection measures in place

## 12. Changes to Privacy Policy

- We may update this policy at any time
- Changes posted on this page
- Email notification for significant changes
- Continued use means acceptance

## 13. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach:
- We will notify affected users within 72 hours
- Notification via email and in-app message
- Details of breach and steps taken
- Recommended actions for users

## 14. Your Consent

By using FaddedSMS, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
